Privacy

Support-page analytics

Only after you choose “Allow analytics” on the Polaris support page, the page loads Google Analytics 4 to measure two support-page interactions: visits arriving through the Polaris extension support link and clicks on the video loading play button. Automatic page views are disabled.

We do not send AI conversation content, extension settings, or PayPal payment details to Google Analytics. Your choice is saved locally in your browser and can be changed from “Analytics settings” in the support-page footer.

Payments and external links

The PayPal button opens PayPal in a new tab. Any payment information you provide is handled by PayPal under its own privacy terms.

Google Analytics

Google may process information according to its Privacy Policy. You can decline analytics and continue using the support page.
