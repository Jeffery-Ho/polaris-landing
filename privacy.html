Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

This policy covers the Polaris browser extension and website. Conversation text used for navigation, search, copying, and export is processed on your device and is not uploaded to Polaris servers. License and payment records, described below, do not include that chat content. The website analytics disclosures supplement the extension’s data-handling policy. This policy is a disclosure of data handling for users in many countries. It is not a warranty that using Polaris complies with any AI provider’s terms, and it is not a warranty that your own use of a third-party chat complies with every country’s AI-platform rules or data laws. Mandatory local privacy rights still apply where the law gives them. Responsibility for provider terms is in the Terms of Use. Who handles license records, and which regional rights may apply, is in section 12.

1. Information We Process

Website Content

Polaris: AI Chat Navigator accesses the text and document structure of AI-generated responses displayed on supported websites.

How Polaris reads a page. On a supported AI chat site, Polaris injects a content script into the tab you already opened. It inspects the page DOM—the same rendered messages you can see—to find headings, lists, and message boundaries, then builds local navigation markers (TOC), an Outline tree, and optional full-text search over what has already been loaded. This inspection happens in your browser. Polaris does not ask the AI provider for a separate OAuth or developer-API grant to read chat text for navigation.

Standard mode only uses content that is already present in the page and removes messages from its index when the website unmounts them. The optional Long conversation mode keeps already loaded messages in the current page's memory for navigation, search and export. If you explicitly turn on automatic backfill, Polaris renders earlier messages of your own open conversation that the website has already delivered to the browser, using only the website's own page mechanics (scrolling its message list or pressing its own "load earlier" control) while the list is covered, then restores your reading position. Shared conversations are never backfilled, and backfill pauses while the page is hidden. This automated scrolling is disclosed here and in the Terms of Use; a provider may still treat it as restricted automation. You decide whether to turn it on.

The index is cleared on conversation changes, page refresh or closure, or returning to Standard mode. Hidden reasoning, tool messages, alternate branches, and downloaded attachments are not indexed. This access is necessary to identify headings and list structures, generate navigation markers, scroll to selected sections, show AI response content in the full-screen text-selection feature, and support user-initiated conversation copying and file export.

Website content is processed locally within the user's browser. Conversation text is not transmitted to the developer, to a Polaris server, or to any other server for Polaris to process. The license, trial, purchase, and recovery requests described below do not include AI conversations, prompts, or page content. Polaris does not call website APIs, does not request or use your account session, authentication tokens or credentials, and does not intercept or modify website requests. Polaris does not ask for passwords. On startup, the extension deletes the conversation cache database used by earlier versions without reading its contents; website storage is not changed.

Third-party page injection

Host permissions let Polaris run only on disclosed AI chat origins. The extension inserts its own UI (markers, Outline, settings entry points) into those pages. Some providers' terms discourage or prohibit unauthorized plugins and page overlays. Polaris discloses this injection so you can decide whether to enable the extension on each site. Conversation text used for navigation is still processed locally as described above.

Extension Settings

Polaris: AI Chat Navigator stores user preferences such as top and right spacing, the maximum number of visible markers, tooltip width, enabled heading levels, and unordered-list marker preferences. The reading mode (Standard or Long conversation) and the automatic backfill choice are synchronized with the other preferences. No conversation content is stored in these settings.

These settings are stored using the Chrome Storage Sync API. If browser synchronization is enabled, the settings may be synchronized across the user's signed-in Chrome browsers by Google under Google's applicable privacy terms. The developer cannot access these synchronized settings.

Trials, Purchases and Licenses

Polaris can contact its license service to initialize a seven-day trial, obtain available plans and validate an existing license. It generates a random installation identifier and independent credential; these, device activation credentials and pending purchase claims are stored in Chrome Storage Local and are not synchronized. Free platform choices stay on the device. No Polaris account or hardware fingerprint is required.

When you choose to purchase, Stripe-hosted Checkout processes payment and collects your checkout email. Card information is not stored by the extension or license service. The Cloudflare Worker and D1 service store the order, license and activation records needed for fulfillment and validation. License and activation verifiers are hashed; email and license delivery material are encrypted separately for delivery and recovery. License delivery uses the disclosed license service. Neither payment nor licensing includes AI conversations, prompts, browsing history, full browsing URLs or hardware fingerprints.

A recovery request uses the checkout email and returns the same confirmation whether or not a matching purchase exists. The service uses short-lived hashed-IP counters and delivery throttling to limit abuse. The test integration is separate from a production billing rollout, and local UI previews do not charge a payment method.

Diagnostic Logs

If the user explicitly selects a diagnostic action in About & Settings, Polaris: AI Chat Navigator records a bounded session-only log for the current tab. The log contains the extension version, coarse platform and browser summaries, viewport measurements, pointer-drag lifecycle outcomes, route and render counts, and error type names. It is designed to exclude AI response text, marker titles, full URLs, account information, prompts, cookies, tokens, error messages, and stack traces. Conversation history is never loaded for diagnostics.

The log is stored only in the current tab's session storage until the tab is closed or the session is cleared. "Download only" saves a redacted JSON file locally. "Send diagnostic log" also opens a pre-addressed email draft to jefferyho.build@gmail.com; the browser does not automatically attach the local file, so the user chooses whether to attach and send it. Polaris: AI Chat Navigator does not upload diagnostic logs or add a background upload service.

Clipboard

When the user activates a copy action, Polaris: AI Chat Navigator writes the selected text, a chapter, or conversation text to the system clipboard. Conversation copying includes the user messages and AI replies that have been read from the page and is independent of chapter filters or folding. While Long conversation mode is still indexing or backfilling, copying and export wait until it finishes; otherwise they include only content already read from the page. The extension does not read existing clipboard content or upload copied text. Clipboard contents are managed by the user's operating system and any clipboard tools the user has enabled.

Conversation File Export

After copying a conversation, the user can explicitly save the same message scope as Markdown or HTML. Files are generated locally in the browser and downloaded to a location managed by the user; conversation text is not sent to a processing server. Exported images are represented by text labels, and raw message HTML is sanitized or escaped. Normal links remain clickable; following them contacts their destination. Downloaded files remain until the user deletes them. Any sharing or cloud backup of these files is controlled by the user and the applications or services they choose.

4. Data Retention

Polaris does not persist AI responses or user prompts. The conversation index and any messages retained by Long conversation mode live only in the current page's memory and are cleared on conversation changes, page refresh or closure, or returning to Standard mode. No conversation content is written to local storage, IndexedDB, or synchronization.

Extension settings remain in Chrome storage until they are reset or removed by the user through the browser or extension settings. Synced settings may also be managed through the user's Google Chrome synchronization settings.

Diagnostic events remain in session storage for the current tab, are bounded in count and size, and are cleared when the tab session ends or when the browser clears session storage. A downloaded copy remains under the user's control, and any email retention is governed by the user's mail provider and choices.

Local installation and activation credentials remain until the extension is removed or its local storage is cleared. Device deactivation revokes its activation credential but retains the installation identity and trial deadline. Test license delivery ciphertext and order records remain for the lifetime of that test license; delivery retry metadata does not contain plaintext credentials. Short-lived rate-limit counters are periodically deleted. Contact the developer about deleting server-side test purchase and license records. You may email support@polaris-ai.work to ask for deletion of server-side purchase and license records we still hold. Where we can, and where tax, accounting, fraud-prevention, or another legal duty does not require us to keep them, we will delete or de-identify them. Polaris keeps no conversation content on our servers or in a persistent cache on your device, so there is no conversation data to delete through that request.

8. User Choices

You may also reset other extension preferences from the extension settings, clear the current tab's diagnostic session by closing the tab or clearing session storage, and manage synchronized settings through Chrome synchronization. 隐私政策

更新日期：2026 年 11 月 12 日

本政策涵盖 Polaris 浏览器扩展与官网。用于导航、搜索、复制和导出的会话文本在用户设备上处理，不上传到 Polaris 服务器。下文所述许可和支付记录不包含这些会话内容。下方官网分析说明补充扩展的数据处理政策。本政策是面向许多国家用户的数据处理披露。它不是使用 Polaris 即符合任何 AI 平台条款的保证，也不是用户自己使用第三方对话即符合每一国家的 AI 平台规则或数据法律的保证。法律赋予的当地隐私权利仍然适用。遵守平台条款的责任见使用条款。许可记录由谁处理、以及可能适用的地区权利，见第 12 节。

1. 我们处理的信息

会话内容与长对话模式

Polaris 在浏览器本地读取受支持 AI 网页的回复内容与文档结构，用于目录、导航、搜索和阅读。

页面是怎么读的。在受支持的 AI 对话网站上，Polaris 向用户已经打开的标签页注入内容脚本。它查看页面 DOM，也就是用户能看见的已渲染消息，找出标题、列表和消息边界，再在本地生成导航标记（目录）、大纲树，以及对已经加载内容的可选全文搜索。这些检查发生在用户的浏览器里。Polaris 不会另向 AI 厂商申请 OAuth 或开发者 API 授权，来读取对话文本做导航。

标准模式只使用页面上已经出现的内容，网站移除某条消息后同步从索引中移除。可选的长对话模式会把已加载的消息保留在当前页面的内存中，用于导航、搜索和导出。用户明确开启自动补齐后，Polaris 只用网站自身的页面机制（滚动其消息列表或点击其自带的“加载更早消息”控件），在遮罩下让网站已下发到浏览器、属于用户本人当前对话的更早消息显示出来，结束后恢复阅读位置。分享页不补齐，页面隐藏时暂停。这种自动滚动在本政策和使用条款中说明，平台仍可能将其视为受限制的自动化。是否开启，由用户自己决定。

索引在切换会话、刷新或关闭页面、切回标准模式时清除。隐藏推理、工具消息、其他分支和下载的附件不纳入索引。

会话正文只在浏览器本地处理，不发送给开发者，也不发送到 Polaris 服务器或任何由 Polaris 处理正文的服务器。下文的许可、试用、购买和找回请求不包含 AI 会话、提示词或页面正文。Polaris 不调用网站接口，不请求或使用账号登录会话、鉴权令牌或凭据，也不拦截或修改网站请求；Polaris 不索要密码。

会话内容不写入本地存储、IndexedDB 或同步。扩展启动时会直接删除旧版本使用的会话缓存数据库，不读取其内容；网站自身的存储不受影响。

第三方页面注入

网站权限只允许 Polaris 在已披露的 AI 对话网站上运行。扩展会把自己的界面（标记、大纲、设置入口）插入这些页面。部分平台条款不鼓励或禁止未经授权的插件和页面覆盖层。Polaris 披露这种注入，便于用户决定是否在各个网站上启用扩展。用于导航的对话文本仍按上文在本地处理。

扩展设置

间距、显示数量、提示宽度、识别范围等偏好存于 Chrome Storage Sync。读取方式（标准模式或长对话模式）和自动补齐选择随其他偏好同步，偏好中不包含会话内容。同步由 Google 按其适用政策处理，开发者不能访问同步设置。

试用、购买与许可证

扩展可联系许可服务以初始化七天试用、获取方案和验证许可证。随机安装标识、独立凭据、设备激活凭据及待确认购买信息存于 Chrome Storage Local，不同步；免费平台选择留在本机。无需 Polaris 账号，不使用硬件指纹。

用户选择购买后，由 Stripe 托管 Checkout 处理支付并收集结账邮箱，扩展与许可服务不保存银行卡信息。Cloudflare Worker 和 D1 保存履约、验证所需的订单、许可及激活记录；许可和激活验证值经过哈希处理，邮箱与许可交付材料分别加密。许可证交付由已披露的许可服务完成。这些流程不包含 AI 会话、提示词、浏览历史、完整浏览网址或硬件指纹。

找回请求使用结账邮箱，无论是否存在匹配购买均返回相同确认；短期 IP 哈希计数与发送限速用于防滥用。测试集成与正式收款发布分开，本地界面预览不会扣款。

诊断日志

用户在设置中主动选择诊断操作后，扩展为当前标签页记录有条数和大小上限的会话日志，包括版本、粗粒度平台与浏览器信息、视口、指针拖动结果、路由和渲染次数、错误类型。诊断从不加载会话历史。白名单设计排除正文、目录标题、完整网址、账号、提示词、Cookie、令牌、错误消息和堆栈。

日志仅存于当前标签页 sessionStorage。“仅下载”保存脱敏 JSON；“发送诊断日志”另打开预填至 jefferyho.build@gmail.com 的邮件草稿，不自动附加文件，由用户自行决定附件和发送。扩展不自动上传日志。

剪贴板与导出

用户主动复制时，扩展将所选文本、本章或会话文本写入系统剪贴板，不读取原有剪贴板、不上传复制正文。会话复制包含已从页面读取的用户消息与 AI 回复，不受章节筛选或折叠影响；长对话模式仍在整理或补齐时，需等待完成后才能复制和导出，其余情况仅包含已读取内容。剪贴板由系统及用户启用的剪贴板工具管理。

复制后可主动保存相同范围的 Markdown 或 HTML，由浏览器本地生成下载，不发送至处理服务器。图片转为文字标签，原始 HTML 经清洗或转义；普通链接仍可点击，点击后会访问其目标。下载文件由用户保存和删除；分享及云备份由用户选择的应用或服务处理。

4. 保留与清除

Polaris 不持久化 AI 回复或提示词。会话索引及长对话模式保留的消息只存在于当前页面的内存中，切换会话、刷新或关闭页面、切回标准模式时清除；会话内容不写入本地存储、IndexedDB 或同步。

扩展设置保留至重置、清除或卸载；同步设置也可在浏览器同步设置中管理。诊断日志随标签页会话结束或 sessionStorage 清除而删除；已下载文件及已发送邮件由用户和邮件服务商管理。

本机安装和激活凭据保留至卸载或清除本地存储。停用设备会撤销激活凭据，但保留安装标识和试用截止时间。测试许可证交付密文及订单记录保留至该测试许可证生命周期结束；邮件重试元数据不含明文凭据，短期限速计数定期删除。可联系开发者申请删除服务器上的测试购买和许可记录。用户也可以发邮件至 support@polaris-ai.work，申请删除我们仍保存的服务器端购买和许可记录。在能够删除，且税务、会计、防欺诈或其他法律义务不要求继续保存时，我们会删除或去标识这些记录。Polaris 不在服务器或设备上持久保存会话内容，因此这项申请不涉及会话数据。

8. 用户选择

停用或卸载 Polaris 即可停止扩展读取页面。要停止保留更早的消息，请在偏好设置中切换到标准模式；如需保留长对话模式但不自动补齐，请关闭自动补齐开关。补齐进行中，在网页上滚动、点击、触摸或按键也会立即停止。“清除并重建当前索引”会丢弃当前对话保留的消息，按页面现有内容重建。切换会话或刷新、关闭页面时，内存中的索引也会清除。

用户还可以在扩展设置中重置其他偏好，通过关闭标签页或清除 sessionStorage 清除当前标签页的诊断会话，并在 Chrome 同步设置中管理已同步的设置。诊断分享、复制和文件导出只在用户主动操作时发生。这些选择只控制用户设备上的 Polaris，不表示平台把此前的使用视为已被允许。遵守平台规则的责任见使用条款。官网分析可拒绝并随时调整。
