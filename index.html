Your conversations stay private. Nothing you read is uploaded.

Some AI chat extensions send your conversations to their own servers. Polaris never does.

It all happens in your browser

Polaris reads the answer on the page you have open and builds its navigation right there. What you read is never sent to us or to any third party.

Saved here, never synced

When complete indexing keeps a copy of a conversation, it stays in this browser only. It is never synced to other devices or uploaded.

You stay in control

Polaris never asks for a password. This website only measures visits after you allow it, and you can change that anytime.

A few things to know

What happens after my trial?

Your 7-day trial starts when you first open Polaris. After it ends, you switch to Free with any two platforms. You won’t be charged, and your local data stays on your device.

Does lifetime access need renewing?

No. Pro is a one-time purchase, with no subscription or automatic renewal.

Can I use Pro on another device?

Yes. Enter your license key in Polaris on your other devices. There is no device limit. You can redeem or recover your license from Settings → Get Pro.

Install Polaris AI

Choose the installation method that works best for you.

Local installation Recommended in Mainland China

Download the ZIP, unzip it, then load the folder in Chrome extensions.

Download ZIP Downloading…

ZIP-version concept demo. TOC (table of contents) and Outline correspond to Navigation and Chapters in the extension.

  1. Download and unzip the offline ZIP package.
  2. Open chrome://extensions and turn on Developer mode.
  3. Click Load unpacked and select the unzipped Polaris folder.
  4. Refresh the AI chat page to start using Polaris.
